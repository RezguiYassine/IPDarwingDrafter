# Release 2026-09-28 — first training corpus

**1,269 vectorized engineering drawings**, each an SVG/DXF pair produced from a
patent TIF, each carrying a sealed acceptance record that re-verifies under
the frozen deployment identity below.

Status: **released on the owner's audit verdict of 2026-09-29 — "the pipeline
is deployable, the geometry is correct."**

## How the corpus was selected

1. **Scope.** Patents whose primary IPC section is B or F: 4,654 of 15,606.
2. **Content.** 2,030 figures judged by the owner with `tools/curate_pilot.py`.
   A second pass with `tools/revisit_rejects.py` overturned 465 of 475
   rejections given as `shaded_render_or_photo`, which on inspection were
   perspective and isometric line art rather than photographs. 1,465 in scope.
3. **Pipeline.** Stages 0–4 under the frozen identity, one figure per patent.
4. **Acceptance.** Nine checks, fail-closed. A figure is training-eligible only
   when all nine pass.
5. **Selection.** `tools/build_training_manifest.py --trust-acceptance`: the
   contract alone decides. The tool's legacy content filter, which predates
   the contract and rejected 1,464 of 1,465 figures, is bypassed.

## K2 — the owner's audit

100 of the 1,269, drawn at random on a fixed seed, judged by eye with the
reconstruction beside the source. Stop criteria were set before the audit.

| criterion, set in advance | result | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| more than 10 of 100 unusable | **0 unusable** — 85 usable, 15 needs review | pass |
| more than 2% of ~200 numerals wrong | **not measured** — see below | open |
| any single pattern on ≥ 5 figures | **thick strokes on 12 figures** | triggered, investigated, accepted |

**What the audit supports.** No figure in the sample was unusable. With 0 of 100
failing, the 95% upper bound on the unusable rate across the release is about
3%.

**The pattern criterion triggered, and was resolved by the owner.** Thick
strokes, black areas and solid-black hatching appeared on 12 of 100 figures.
That is a real, recurring defect (limitation 1 below). It was investigated
before release and the owner judged the corpus deployable with it recorded.

**The numeral audit was not performed on the release.** The contact-sheet
instrument (`tools/build_label_audit.py`) was built and demonstrated on the
predecessor run, where 20 of 20 numerals on the sampled sheet were correct,
but the owner's audit covered geometry. The wrong-text rate of the released
labels is therefore not measured by a person. The vocabulary gate detects a
numeral the patent never names — 3,919 of 18,675 readings, 21.0%, were demoted
to unidentified marks rather than written — but it cannot detect a wrong
reading that happens to be a valid numeral. **Treat reference text in this
release as unverified.**

## Known limitations — from the audit

### 1. Thick strokes, black areas, solid hatching — 12 of 100

The owner's note: *"the original drawing presents thick strokes, which leads
our pipeline to produce no clean render"* — where "thick" also covers solid
black regions and hatching filled solid. The pipeline skeletonizes ink to a
centreline; a stroke many pixels wide, or a filled region, skeletonizes to a
branching medial axis rather than the outline a draughtsman meant.

Estimated share of the release: **12%**, roughly 6–20% at 95% confidence, so
~80–250 figures. No automatic detector separates them: a stroke-width
statistic catches 4 of the 12 at a threshold that also flags 3 of 88 good
figures. They cannot be filtered out of this release after the fact.

### 2. Out-of-distribution hatching — 2 of 100

Hatch styles outside HatchUNet's training distribution. The same blind spot
the owner flagged on 2026-09-17, now observed in the release. Phase C's 98.2%
hatch recall does not speak to it: that was measured on synthetic sheets drawn
at a single thin stroke width.

### 3. Ghost leader lines — about one leader in three

The owner's note: leader lines are duplicated, most visibly where the original
is curved — the reconstruction shows a straight thin stroke beside the curve.

Measured on 2,611 removed leaders in the release:

| | share | chord lying on source ink | ink left behind after removal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| straight leaders | 53.8% | ≥ 90% | **0%** |
| curved leaders | 34.9% | < 60% | **75%** |

Mechanism: Stage 0 traces a leader as a straight segment and masks a straight
band along it. A curved leader escapes the band, so three quarters of its ink
survives into Stage 2 and is vectorized as geometry. Stage 4 then reinjects
the leader annotation as the straight segment. Both appear in the output.

This is a Stage-0 defect with a clear fix — trace curved leaders along the
ink rather than as a chord — and it is the first item of the next roadmap.

## Known limitations — carried forward

- **Reference text is demoted, not corrected**, on 21% of readings. Those
  marks are removed and preserved as crops, and carry no text link.
- **Annotation geometry is out of the fidelity target** by the owner's
  decision of 2026-09-21. Arrowheads, dimension lines and leaders reconstruct
  at 67–71% recall against 92–98% for drawing geometry.
- **Every ground-truth fidelity number is synthetic.** Recall 0.957 and
  precision 0.951 on the drawing-only target, over 498 synthetic sheets. The
  audit is the only measurement on real output.
- **Electrical drawings are excluded** by the IPC scope. Sections G and H held
  about a third of the curated drawings.
- **Content is human-decided.** Four classifier attempts peaked at 94.0%
  precision against a 98% requirement, so every figure in this release is one
  a person chose.
- **The isolation gate rejects healthy graphs.** 48 of 49 Stage-2 rejections
  in the pre-release run tripped isolation rather than fragmentation, on
  figures with normal edge length and micro ratio — the defect `max_edges`
  had, in the gate that replaced it. The excluded figures are recoverable.

## Reproducing

```bash
python -m tools.batch_run \
  --worklist benchmarks/mechanical/worklist_full.csv \
  --config config_release_2026-09-28.yaml \
  --output output/RELEASE_2026-09-28 \
  --content-manifest benchmarks/mechanical/content_decisions.json

python -m tools.build_training_manifest \
  --db output/RELEASE_2026-09-28/results.db \
  --config config_release_2026-09-28.yaml \
  --run-output output/RELEASE_2026-09-28 --trust-acceptance \
  --output-csv releases/2026-09-28/training_manifest.csv
```

Requires the three model files at the hashes above and the implementation
files unchanged. Any deviation is refused at preflight rather than producing a
silently different corpus.
